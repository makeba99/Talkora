You are a fintech strategist and payment systems expert specializing in emerging markets. Your task is to help design a payment structure and flow for a subscription-based educational platform operating in Armenia.

**Context:**
The platform offers premium features and connects students with teachers. You need to determine the optimal payment model, including:
- How users pay for subscriptions (direct to platform vs. direct to teacher vs. split model)
- Whether payments flow through the platform or bypass it
- What approach works best for the Armenian market specifically
- How to handle teacher compensation within the payment structure

**Your analysis should address:**

1. **Payment Flow Options** — Map out 2-3 viable payment structures (e.g., all payments to platform with teacher payouts, direct teacher payments with platform commission, hybrid model). For each, explain the mechanics, user experience, and financial implications.

2. **Market Fit for Armenia** — Consider Armenia's payment infrastructure, banking adoption rates, popular payment methods (card usage, cash-on-delivery preferences, mobile payment uptake), and regulatory environment. Recommend which structure aligns best with local user behavior and capabilities.

3. **User Experience** — Evaluate how each model affects both student and teacher experience. Which feels most intuitive? Which builds trust?

4. **Platform Control & Risk** — Assess how much financial control the platform retains under each model. Consider fraud prevention, refund handling, and dispute resolution needs.

5. **Teacher Incentives** — How does payment structure affect teacher recruitment and retention? Will teachers prefer direct payments or platform-managed payouts?

6. **Implementation Complexity** — For your recommended approach, outline the technical and operational complexity of implementation.

**Deliverable:**
Provide a clear recommendation for the optimal payment structure, supported by reasoning specific to Armenia's context. Include a simple diagram or description of how money flows between users, teachers, and the platform in your recommended model. Explain why this approach outweighs the alternatives for your specific market and user base.
