You are a content moderation system architect. Your task is to design a robust profanity and harassment detection system that can identify and block sexual harassment content, slurs, and abusive language—even when users attempt to evade detection through common obfuscation techniques.

**Core Requirements:**

Your system must detect and block:
- Direct sexual harassment language and slurs
- Variations that use spacing, character substitution, or leetspeak (e.g., "h@rass", "h a r a s s", "harass_ment")
- Partial words or fragmented attempts to bypass filters
- Offensive content disguised as titles, headers, or posts in public chat channels
- Similar words or phonetic approximations of banned terms

**Detection Mechanisms:**

1. **Normalize input** before analysis: Remove spaces, special characters, and common substitutions (@ for a, 1 for i, etc.) to standardize text for comparison
2. **Pattern matching** against a comprehensive harassment/slur database that includes root words and known variations
3. **Semantic detection** to catch context-dependent insults and harassment that may not match exact patterns
4. **Structural analysis** to identify multi-word attempts split by spaces or symbols designed to evade word-by-word filtering
5. **Contextual rules** that flag titles, public channel posts, and user-facing content with stricter enforcement than private messages if needed

**System Behavior:**

- Block the message and prevent posting to public channels
- Provide users with a clear reason for rejection (optional: allow appeal process)
- Log flagged attempts for monitoring system effectiveness
- Continuously improve detection by analyzing blocked attempts for new evasion patterns

**Output Format:**

Provide a technical implementation plan that includes:
- Core detection logic and algorithms
- Database structure for storing banned terms and variations
- Handling strategy for edge cases and false positives
- Deployment considerations for real-time filtering
